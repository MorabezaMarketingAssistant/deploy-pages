02 · Positioning

The right market position

For couples, diaspora return visitors and private groups seeking a more intimate São Vicente experience, Monte Madeiral Village is the boutique hillside stay and social venue that combines privacy, Cape Verdean atmosphere, dining and memorable occasions.

Different from city hotels and commodity rentals

Why this position works

  • It fits the physical concept better than “standard hotel” messaging.
  • It supports premium perception without pretending to be luxury-for-luxury’s-sake.
  • It creates room for dining and event revenue, not just accommodation demand.
  • It gives the brand a more memorable story in a fragmented market.
03 · Revenue model

  • Food & Beverage

  • Private Occasions

04 · What not to do

Commercial traps to avoid

  • Do not position as a budget or mass-market hotel.
  • Do not compete on price before trust and product story are clear.
  • Do not rely on room nights alone.
  • Do not pour budget into broad Google Ads before launch assets are ready.
  • Do not allow slow WhatsApp follow-up to kill high-intent enquiries.
05 · Priority audiences

Who should we actively sell to?

Boutique escape couples

Travellers who shortlist on photos, atmosphere, dining and reviews. Best fit for direct stay positioning.

Diaspora return visitors

People coming back for family, holidays or celebrations who want privacy, pride and a place to gather.

Private occasion organisers

Locals and diaspora families looking for a small venue with food, atmosphere and easier logistics.

Culture-led travellers

Guests who want a base that feels part of São Vicente, not a generic transit stay.

06 · Channel strategy

The launch stack

The launch sequence needs to feel clear at a glance. Each channel has a job, but they should be activated in the right order so the property does not waste money before trust assets are ready.

  • SEO should become Channel 1 over time because it compounds and reduces long-term OTA dependency.
  • OTAs + Google Maps are essential at launch because they lend trust before brand pull exists.
  • Meta Ads + WhatsApp should drive awareness, private occasion enquiries and retargeting.
  • Organic social should reinforce atmosphere, reviews, behind-the-scenes proof and event inspiration.

Owner takeaway

This is not a “run ads and hope” launch. The order matters, and each step needs enough room to breathe on the page:

  • Build trust assets first.
  • Capture enquiries cleanly.
  • Then scale the channels that prove qualified demand.

08 · Paid media

What paid should do

Create awareness

Lead with strong visual storytelling that sells atmosphere, privacy and occasion value.

Drive conversations

Use paid traffic to generate WhatsApp conversations for events and private occasions.

Retarget intent

Retarget people who visited the website, engaged with content or watched videos.

09 · First 90 days

What needs to happen first

Month 1

Photo/video shoot, website launch, OTA setup, Google Business Profile, WhatsApp and tracking setup.

Months 1–3

SEO fundamentals, core pages, launch social content, awareness campaigns, review SOP.

Months 2–3

Events/private occasions page, WhatsApp-led campaign, refine packages and response flow.

Month 3+

Retargeting, review-led optimisation, package testing, better direct capture.

If launch assets are weak, all later marketing becomes less efficient.
10 · Operational risk

The biggest non-marketing risk is response speed.

  • A strong campaign fails if WhatsApp and event enquiries sit unanswered.
  • The business needs fast, warm, commercially confident follow-up during business hours.
  • Review generation must be operational, not occasional.

Minimum operating standard

  • Reply to hot enquiries within 1 hour.
  • Have event package examples ready.
  • Track WhatsApp clicks, calls and forms.
  • Ask every satisfied guest for a review.
11 · Success metrics

What the owner should monitor monthly

Revenue quality

Occupancy, ADR, package uptake, event revenue, direct share.

Demand generation

Organic sessions, brand search, Meta enquiry cost, WhatsApp qualified leads.

Trust signals

Review count, average rating, photo/video quality, landing page performance.

Decision triggers

If occupancy is under 20% by Month 4 or reviews drop below 4.3, adjust fast.

12 · Final recommendation

The smart launch path

  • Launch as a boutique compound with a clear emotional and commercial story.
  • Use OTAs and Google to build trust, but invest early in direct infrastructure.
  • Treat events and dining as core revenue lines, not side products.
  • Protect pricing. Competing on cheapness weakens the whole model.
